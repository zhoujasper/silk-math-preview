<h2 id="english">English</h2>

Open a <code>.tex</code>, Markdown, or Jupyter file and put the caret inside a formula. A preview appears beside it; an orange hairline marks where you are typing. No compile step.

Your own macros, environments, colors, and tables show up too. Undefined commands appear in red; the rest of the formula still renders.

- **Toggle** <code>Ctrl+Alt+M</code> (Mac: <code>Cmd+Alt+M</code>). <code>Esc</code> dismisses the overlay.
- **Settings** click <strong>Silk Math</strong> in the status bar.
- **OCR** click the camera icon. Models download on first use and stay on your machine.

<h2 id="chinese">中文</h2>

打开 <code>.tex</code>、Markdown 或 Jupyter，把光标放进公式里。预览会出现在旁边，橙色细线标出你正在写的位置。不用编译，边写边看。

文档里自己的宏、环境、颜色和表格会一起显示。未定义的命令标成红色，其余部分照常渲染。

- **开关预览** <code>Ctrl+Alt+M</code>（Mac：<code>Cmd+Alt+M</code>），关掉当前浮层按 <code>Esc</code>
- **设置** 点状态栏的 <strong>Silk Math</strong>
- **截图识别** 点状态栏的相机图标。模型首次使用才下载，只在本机运行
